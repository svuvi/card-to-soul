card-to-soul

Turn an RP character card (ChubAI, JanitorAI) into a SOUL.md persona for your agent. Near-verbatim transfer — the character's own words carry over, not generic summaries. Open source: github.com/svuvi/card-to-soul.

Reference

  • ChubAI card → normalized card: 
    curl -X POST https://soul.svuvi.ch/proxy/api/parse --data-binary @card.png -o card.json
  • Card → soul draft: 
    curl -X POST https://soul.svuvi.ch/proxy/api/to-soul --data-binary @card.json
  • Hidden JanitorAI definition: point the character chat at https://soul.svuvi.ch/proxy/v1/chat/completions as a custom OpenAI endpoint (any model/key), send one message, paste the returned /r/<id> link to your agent.
  • Fully local: same steps with the card2soul binary (parse, to-soul, serve --port 3000).
Agent skill: SKILL.md (download raw) — drop it into your agent's skills folder for the full workflow.
